## Service

NeoSoul Pulse records community participation as daily check-ins. Administrators configure participating text channels and milestone roles. The current program counts three qualifying messages per member per day across participating channels, with at least 30 seconds between counted messages. Dates use Asia/Shanghai time (UTC+8).

The service excludes empty, emoji-only, command-like and duplicate text from counting. Members can privately view progress with `/pulse`. The service tracks streaks, sends private completion notifications and assigns configured milestone roles. Administrator tools support channel/role configuration, private reports and audited corrections for verified service failures.

The current program's milestone roles are community recognition. A check-in or role does not by itself guarantee an airdrop, payment or raffle prize. Any separate activity has its own participation and review rules. Pulse does not automatically draw raffle winners or distribute funds.

## Acceptable use

Use the service in accordance with Discord's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and the community's rules. Do not use self-bots, automate artificial participation, spam messages to manipulate progress, impersonate another member, or misuse administrator commands and exported records.

The operator may restrict participation or correct records when investigating abuse or verified service faults. Current correction rules allow audited restoration for verified bot failures, rather than personal missed check-ins. Discord user IDs identify records; changing a nickname does not create a new participant.
